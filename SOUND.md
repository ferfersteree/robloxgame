# V20: sound, music and fixes

`SpearHunt V21 - Final.rbxl` (V20 plus the id hookup below) is V19 plus the sound system, flipped owner signs and fixes from review.

## Sound
- **72 new files** in `audio/ogg/` (UI, rewards, footsteps, spear, training, dialog, big moments, 3 music tracks, forest ambience). `audio/manifest.json` lists each file with its length, loop flag and what it is for. They are generated by code (`audio/tools/`, Python with numpy and scipy), so any of them can be redone: softer, brighter, shorter.
- **How the game uses them.** `Client/Sfx` now has sound banks: one event, several variants played in rotation (never the same twice in a row) with a small random pitch. Hover and press use the soft glass tick and warm pop. Windows open and close with an air sweep and a chime. New events: spear charge loop, throw, flight whistle (attached to the flying spear and removed on landing), hit on animal and ground, training rep, forge hits, dialog blips, jump and landing.
- **Ready but not used by any game event yet:** `miss`, `pickup`, `select`, `back`, switch on/off, `hit_wood` (only in the forge), `coin_burst`. They are in the bank so a button or event can call `Sfx.play("select")` and so on.
- **Footsteps.** New `Client/Footsteps`: steps by surface (grass / stone / wood, 4 variants each), jump and landing. It replaces the default Roblox step sounds only when the custom ones are loaded.
- **Music.** Calm tracks in normal play, a livelier one while hunting, with a smooth crossfade when you start or stop hunting. Catch reveals and windows keep the current track (they do not restart it). If one of your uploaded tracks fails to load, the game moves on to the others or to the old library. A quiet forest ambience bed runs under them.
- **Pitch.** Calls that were tuned for the old built-in sounds (coins, sparkle, level up) are rescaled against the old default pitch and limited to 0.6x to 1.7x, so the new files do not sound chirpy or muddy.
- **Listen first:** the Sound Lab page has every sound and track.

### One step is still needed: upload (scripted: `tools/upload_to_roblox.py`, see README.md)
Roblox only plays audio that was uploaded and has an id. Until the ids are filled in, the game keeps using its built-in sounds exactly as before. This was tested: 87 sound calls (every changed call site and the unchanged ones) were replayed through the V18 `Sfx` and the V20 `Sfx` with an empty `AudioIds`, and the sample, pitch and volume of every play are identical (`tools/merge/harness/run_eq.lua`). To switch the new sounds on:
1. Creator Hub > Assets > upload every file from `audio/ogg/` as **Audio**.
2. Put each id into `Client/AudioIds` (`ui_open = "rbxassetid://123..."`). The names are the file names without `.ogg`.
Uploading needs an ID-verified account and Roblox moderates audio, so it can take some minutes. The whole upload can also be scripted with an Open Cloud API key.

## Owner signs
The owner name sign at each yard gate (`Plots/PlotN/OwnerSign`) faced the house. Its text is now on the opposite face, toward the exit, so it reads from outside the yard. Only the `Face` of its SurfaceGui changed. The sign on the house wall already faced outward.

## World collisions
Checked 51,431 parts of the map. Result: mostly by design, no hard bug.
- Solid structure is collidable (logs, roofs, gables, barrels, beams). Pure decoration is walk-through on purpose: crystals (371), floating rings, water, clouds, pedestals and columns in the showroom, plot gate posts.
- **Invisible walls:** exactly four, the village boundary: `Village/ForestWall` (z = -8, 252 wide), two `SideWall` (x = ±214) and `BackWall` (z = 222). They explain "I cannot pass here". If players should walk from the village straight into the forest, `ForestWall` is the one to open or replace with a gate.
- Nothing was changed in the world's collisions.

## Fixes from review of V19
- A throw at the range cap (5800) landed in zone 20, which has no terrain and species no spear can catch. `Config.zoneFor` now keeps throws at or beyond `MAX_THROW_DISTANCE` in zone 19. Zones 20 to 25 open when `MAX_THROW_DISTANCE` is raised (and spears for their tiers and terrain exist). This also fixes the aiming HUD, which showed zones 21 to 25.
- Animals update at 60 Hz when close (as before) and no longer block raycasts (shots, spear splashes).

## V39: sound of the egg opening (70 new sounds, music, preview page)

`SpearHunt V39 - Egg Sounds.rbxl` is V38 plus a sound for every beat of the egg opening. Before, the 3D opening had six sound calls in 6-10 seconds; now every event of the animation has one.

- **Where it lives.** `audio/tools/foley.py` (sound-design primitives: modal synthesis for glass / ceramic / wood / steel / bells, noise transients, micro-crackle, bubbles, swept whooshes, a synthetic room, strings, brass swell, vowel choir, Karplus-Strong harp, timpani), `egg_sounds.py` (effects), `egg_music.py` (stingers, loops, build-up), `egg_cues.py` (the single spec), `egg_build.py` (builds everything). Run `python audio/tools/egg_build.py`; every sound has its own seed, so only a changed recipe changes a file.
- **What the build writes.** `audio/ogg/*.ogg` (the upload script takes them from there), the generated tables inside `Client/Sfx` (banks, fallbacks, volumes) and `Client/EggOpen3D` (cue rules and timetable), the new keys in the `Client/AudioIds` template, `audio/egg_manifest.json` and `preview/egg-sounds.html`.
- **How the game plays it.** `EggOpen3D` hears every event of `EggOpenings` (burst, ring, flash, bolt, stream) and plays the sound that the family's rule says (belt segments splinter, amber segments light up as a rising scale, pearls burst as rising pops, storm clamps release one by one, ...). Scene beats (egg appears, heartbeat knocks, shell opens by rarity, animal emerges, slow motion, light shafts, halo, camera orbit, landing, reveal) come from a timetable with anchors. Long sounds (spark streams, the build-up, the music under the card) are layers with fade in and out. While the scene runs the world music is ducked (`Sfx.duck`) and released at the end.
- **Fallback.** Until a file is uploaded the game plays an existing similar sound (`EGG_ALIAS` in `Sfx`), loops and music stay silent. Every sound call of the scene is wrapped in `pcall`: a sound bug cannot stop the opening. `EggScene` (2D fallback) and the plain 2D hatch in `Shop` got the same sounds in a smaller form.
- **Listening before upload.** Open `preview/egg-sounds.html` in Chrome or Edge: it plays the whole opening (5 eggs x 7 rarities x 3 jump lengths) with every sound at the moment the game plays it, lists all sounds with ▶ and has "like / redo" buttons that collect feedback as text.
- **Upload.** `python tools/upload_to_roblox.py --user-id ID --only audio` uploads only the new files (older ones are in `out/uploaded.json`). Write only the new keys into the `Client/AudioIds` of the place (`tools/patch_many.py` does several modules in one pass); do not paste the whole `out/AudioIds.luau` (its picture ids are empty).
- **Checking Luau without Studio.** `python tools/luau_check.py <files or folders>` is a syntax checker (no downloads, nothing is run); it accepts every .luau of this repository and rejects broken structure. It does not type-check.
- **Not verified.** The scene was not run in Studio and the sounds were not listened to by the author of this version; levels (`vol` in `egg_cues.py`) are a first guess.
