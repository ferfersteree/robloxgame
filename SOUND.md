# V20: sound, music and fixes

`SpearHunt V20 - Sound.rbxl` is V19 plus the sound system, flipped owner signs and fixes from review.

## Sound
- **72 new files** in `audio/ogg/` (UI, rewards, footsteps, spear, training, dialog, big moments, 3 music tracks, forest ambience). `audio/manifest.json` lists each file with its length, loop flag and what it is for. They are generated by code (`audio/tools/`, Python with numpy and scipy), so any of them can be redone: softer, brighter, shorter.
- **How the game uses them.** `Client/Sfx` now has sound banks: one event, several variants played in rotation (never the same twice in a row) with a small random pitch. Hover and press use the soft glass tick and warm pop. Windows open and close with an air sweep and a chime. New events: spear charge loop, throw, flight whistle (attached to the flying spear and removed on landing), hit on animal / ground / wood, training rep, dialog blips, coin burst, jump and landing.
- **Footsteps.** New `Client/Footsteps`: steps by surface (grass / stone / wood, 4 variants each), jump and landing. It replaces the default Roblox step sounds only when the custom ones are loaded.
- **Music.** Calm tracks in normal play, a livelier one while hunting, with a smooth crossfade when the mood changes. A quiet forest ambience bed runs under them.
- **Listen first:** the Sound Lab page has every sound and track.

### One step is still needed: upload
Roblox only plays audio that was uploaded and has an id. Until the ids are filled in, the game keeps using its built-in sounds exactly as before (nothing breaks). To switch the new sounds on:
1. Creator Hub > Assets > upload every file from `audio/ogg/` as **Audio**.
2. Put each id into `Client/AudioIds` (`ui_open = "rbxassetid://123..."`). The names are the file names without `.ogg`.
Uploading needs an ID-verified account and Roblox moderates audio, so it can take some minutes. The whole upload can also be scripted with an Open Cloud API key.

## Owner signs
The owner name sign at each yard gate (`Plots/PlotN/OwnerSign`) faced the house. Its text is now on the opposite face, toward the exit, so it reads from outside the yard. Only the `Face` of its SurfaceGui changed. The sign on the house wall already faced outward.

## World collisions
Checked 51,431 parts of the map. Result: mostly by design, no hard bug.
- Solid structure is collidable (logs, roofs, gables, barrels, beams). Pure decoration is walk-through on purpose: crystals (371), floating rings, water, clouds, pedestals and columns in the showroom, plot gate posts.
- **Invisible walls:** exactly four, the village boundary: `Village/ForestWall` (z = -8, 252 wide), two `SideWall` (x = ±214) and `BackWall` (z = 222). They explain "I cannot pass here". If players should walk from the village straight into the forest, `ForestWall` is the one to open or replace with a gate.
- Nothing was changed in the world's collisions.

## Fixes from review of V19
- A throw at the range cap (5800) landed in zone 20, which has no terrain and species no spear can catch. `Config.zoneFor` now keeps throws at or beyond `MAX_THROW_DISTANCE` in zone 19. Zones 20 to 25 open when `MAX_THROW_DISTANCE` is raised (and spears for their tiers and terrain exist). This also fixes the aiming HUD, which showed zones 21 to 25.
- Animals update at 60 Hz when close (as before) and no longer block raycasts (shots, spear splashes).

## Tools
`tools/v20/edit.rs` applies script edits, new modules and enum properties to a place (`edits.txt`). `tools/v20/mapdump.rs` dumps map parts and signs. `tools/merge/harness/run_sfx.lua` runs `Sfx` headlessly in a Luau interpreter, with and without custom ids.

Not run in Studio. The audio itself could not be listened to by the author of the generator; judge it in the Sound Lab.
