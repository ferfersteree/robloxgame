The gold track can be purchased midseason and unlocks every previously earned premium reward. Keep player level and pass level separate.

Desktop shows five columns per page. Pin the FREE and PREMIUM lane labels while columns scroll together. Put the Missions panel on the right with independently scrollable daily and weekly lists. On a phone, display three columns and switch between Rewards and Missions using tabs. A check means claimed, a ribbon means ready, and a lock shows the tier or premium requirement. Tap a tile for its quantity and claim action. Grant earned, unclaimed rewards once at season close.
